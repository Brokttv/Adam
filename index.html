Adam Elimadi

I'm Adam, born in Boston and raised in Casablanca. I speak four languages fluently (English, French, Darija, Arabic). I graduated high school majoring in Physics and Chemistry with a score of 17.84/20, placing me among the top 1% nationwide. I came back to Boston for the first time in 17 years to pursue higher studies. I arrived with so many expectations, but I was struck by a bit of unforseen events so I had to adapt (still figuring it out).

I got interested in AI mid 2024 and started self-teaching ML early 2025. Beginning in 2026, I started gradually pivoting to AI safety, where I got extremely interested in interpretability and joined MAIA (MIT AI Alignment) as an AISF fellow. After 3 months, I wrote my first paper and submitted it to the COLM Interp workshop after Claude encouraged me to (had no idea what COLM was), and it got accepted. Currently working on an exciting agenda (could robust models possibly be easier to reverse-engineer?) while independently studying neuroscience.

I'm a strong believer that nature has a lot to teach us and big breakthroughs are a product of interdisciplinary efforts, so I hope our brain can show us better ways of untangling those "black boxes".

I am always open to research collaboration & internship opportunities. If you see a fit, please send an email. And if you're around Boston and want to hang out, also reach out. Perhaps strangers are friends we don't know yet (:

What do you optimize for in life?
I see humans as vectors in a high-dimensional space, and that space is intelligence. The coordinates of these vectors refer to all the faculties unique to us (e.g. relationships, character, career, religiosity, deeds, knowledge, morality, etc.). I want my vector to surpass a certain threshold norm spanning the space in the right direction, so when I die, I get to die as a successful human being, not solely as a successful researcher, football player, or businessman.
